    Cardiovascular Health Guidance

    Educational cardiovascular guidance

    🫀 Blood Pressure

    Monitor blood pressure regularly. Consult a physician regarding persistent elevated readings.

    🧪 Cholesterol

    Maintain a diet balanced in healthy fats and schedule regular lipid screenings.

    🏃 Physical Activity

    Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate activity daily as suitable for your baseline.

    🥗 Diet

    Emphasize whole foods, vegetables, high fiber, and reduced dietary sodium.

    🚭 Smoking & Alcohol

    Avoid tobacco products and limit alcohol consumption to minimize vascular stress.

    📋 Regular Check-ups

    Schedule routine medical evaluations with your healthcare provider.
