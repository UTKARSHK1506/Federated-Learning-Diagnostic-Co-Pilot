    Cardiovascular Health Guidance

    General educational information on cardiovascular health factors

    🫀 Blood Pressure

    Monitor your blood pressure regularly. Discuss persistent high readings with a healthcare professional.

    🧪 Cholesterol Management

    Maintain a diet low in saturated fats. Have lipid levels checked as recommended by your physician.

    🏃 Physical Activity

    Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate physical activity daily, appropriate for your medical baseline.

    🥗 Healthy Diet

    Focus on nutrient-dense foods, high fiber, whole grains, and low sodium intake.

    🚭 Smoking & Alcohol

    Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption to reduce vascular stress and risk factors.
